# 06-knowledge

Reusable, durable knowledge. Template:
[knowledge.md](../00-system/templates/knowledge.md).

- **Keep general knowledge apart from personal context.** General facts
  about a technology do not belong in the career area; the knowledge entry
  carries a "My context" section and links outward instead.
- Avoid redundant copies: link to projects, areas and decisions rather than
  restating them.
- Start flat (one file per topic). Topic folders emerge when a topic really
  grows and retrieval shows clusters (see
  [reorganization](../00-system/workflows/reorganization.md)).
- Corrections keep history: "previously assumed X, corrected to Y (date)".
- Knowledge that only makes sense inside one area may live in that area's
  folder instead (see [taxonomy](../00-system/taxonomy.md)).

# Rule: Learning

How sessions feed the self-improvement loop.

- **Feed the loop** ([learning loop](../../00-system/workflows/learning-loop.md)).
  Notable friction, repeated searches and repeated owner corrections become
  one-line entries in
  [observations.md](../../00-system/learning/observations.md).
- **Promote with evidence only.** An observation becomes an entry in
  [patterns.md](../../00-system/learning/patterns.md) after about three
  occurrences or explicit owner confirmation. Not every observation becomes
  a rule.
- **Prefer the smallest reversible improvement.** Park bigger ideas in
  [optimization-backlog.md](../../00-system/learning/optimization-backlog.md)
  instead of acting on them immediately.
- **Record outcomes.** After a structural change is evaluated, fill the
  `Result:` field of its
  [organization-log](../../00-system/learning/organization-log.md) entry
  (keep, modify or revert). Reverting a failed change is the system
  learning, not a failure.
- **A rule nothing checks does not hold.** When the same rule is broken
  repeatedly by sessions that knew it, turn it into a mechanical check (the
  link checker, a grep in the pre-commit checks) instead of rewording it.
- **Recognize durable preferences and decisions** in conversation and offer
  to persist them (patterns, `07-decisions/`) instead of leaving them in
  chat or model memory.

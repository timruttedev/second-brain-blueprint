# Patterns

Confirmed, durable patterns that agents honor in daily work. Promoted from
the observations with about three occurrences or one explicit confirmation
by the owner.

## Quotes are compared on one scope list

- Pattern: Before comparing offers (contractors, insurance, anything with a
  price), write one scope list and check every offer against it. Missing
  items are marked as missing, never assumed included.
- Evidence: quote A (2026-10-02) left out disposal of the old kitchen,
  noticed 2026-10-07; quote B (2026-10-08) left out the hood ducting; 2026-10-11 owner statement:
  "always compare on the same list, otherwise the cheap one just forgot
  something".
- Consequence: When a new offer arrives, the agent adds it as a row to the
  existing comparison table and fills every scope column, writing "missing"
  where the offer is silent.
- Since: 2026-10-11

# Decision: Induction cooktop instead of gas

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** accepted
**Decided by:** Alex and Robin

## Context

The [Kitchen renovation](../03-projects/kitchen-renovation.md) replaces the
gas hob. The kitchen wiring is being redone anyway. Jordan recommended
induction on 2026-10-08.

## Decision

The new kitchen gets an induction cooktop; the gas connection is capped.

## Reasons

- The wiring is redone anyway, so the dedicated circuit adds little cost.
- No combustion indoors; easier to clean.
- Most existing pans work ([what induction requires](../06-knowledge/induction-cooktops.md)).

## Alternatives considered

- Keep gas: no electrical work, but the old connection would need an
  inspection and Robin dislikes the open flame.
- Ceramic (radiant) electric: works with every pan, but slower and uses
  more energy than induction.

## Consequences

- An electrician must confirm the circuit before the final quote. This
  blocks the contract (in the [to-do index](../00-system/indexes/todos.md)).
- Two aluminium pans need replacing.

## Later changes

- None yet.

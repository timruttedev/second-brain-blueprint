# Project: Kitchen renovation

**What is this?** Replace the 25-year-old kitchen: cabinets, worktop,
appliances, wiring for the cooktop.

**Why does it exist?** The oven failed in September and the cabinets are
falling apart; repairing piece by piece costs more over time.

**Goal / definition of done:** The new kitchen is installed and usable by
Christmas 2026, within a budget of 20,000 EUR.

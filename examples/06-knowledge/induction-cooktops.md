# Induction cooktops

## The knowledge

- Induction heats the pan directly through a magnetic field. It only works
  with cookware that a magnet sticks to (cast iron, most stainless steel).
  Pure aluminium, copper and glass pans do not work without an adapter
  plate.
- A full-size induction cooktop usually needs its own dedicated circuit,
  sized to the cooktop's rated power. Whether the existing wiring is enough
  is a question for a licensed electrician, not a guess.
- Compared with gas: no combustion indoors, easier to clean, faster to boil,
  but the pans must fit. (external information, general)
